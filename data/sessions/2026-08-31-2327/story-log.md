## PANEL: story-log

Courts of the Shadow Fey, Session 7. The party continued navigating the Shadow Fey lower court after reaching the palace and learning that court status gates access upward.

Tam tested dueling culture and learned that duels can raise rank, but losses cost rank. The party looked for noncombat routes through the lower court.

Magnolia honored her promise to the kitchens and supplied fifty goodberries, which the head cook crushed into soup. This earned status in the lower court.

Aldric was summoned before King Mago over the wardstone broken by his fireball on the Shadow Road. He inspected the damaged stone, realized it functioned as a message/alarm ward, and repaired/improved it with magic. Ten-Pound Hammer accepted the repair.

King Mago then made Aldric serve as Reader of the Lower Court. Aldric helped resolve the case of Billoc, a mute goblin accused of theft. The party recognized that Billoc could not defend himself while his hands were bound, freed his hands so he could sign, and persuaded Mago there was no proof of theft. Billoc was released and thanked them.

Tam investigated the stables and befriended the Black Prince's hunting dogs with meat and animal speech. He met Kolya, the kennel master, learned Lazulin is the Black Prince's large blue horse, and learned that the Black Prince's hunt is a high-court event using quarry conjured by Count Samus Hollowgrand.

The party also investigated the guard hall and identified Shadow Fey soldiers from Allosis under Captain Memorethian, but learned little else. End-of-session status noted at table: Tam 5, Judith 5, Magnolia 7, Aldric 7.
