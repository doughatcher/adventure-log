## PANEL: story-log

*(Courts of the Shadow Fey — Session resumed 2026-07-27 after ~1 month hiatus. Vargr DM.)*

The Free City of Zobeck has fallen under shadow — not through open war but through the quiet machinery of an older claim. The city held a monarchy until roughly a century ago; what the citizens call "freedom" the Shadow Fey courts call a debt unpaid. The fey granted the right to rule, the monarchy ended without heirs, and now the courts have come to collect.

**Session 1 (pre-hiatus):** The party was in Zobeck when Shadow Fey guards replaced the city watch at the gates — teleporting anyone who approached, non-lethal but absolute. At the Temple of Kors, they found assassins attempting to kill High Priest Oleg and a note in Elvish revoking the temple's operating permission. A shadow fey bard engaged the party in combat, mind-controlling Magnolia Reed to attack her companions; Judith Quiney killed the bard rather than her friend. The party spent time in libraries and eventually received invitations — left on their pillows while they slept, a message about capability — to the ambassador's residence.

**Ambassador encounter:** Glanen Thelamandryne (approximate spelling), ambassador to the shadow fey, received them with hounds at the door. His colleague — an owl on a stand that was actually another fey in disguise — gifted the party bracers of defense. The conversation established:
- Summer Court (Queen) is currently in session, transitioning to Winter Court (King)
- Shadow Fey seasons are not aligned to Material Realm seasons
- The party barely landed on the "friend" side of his friend/enemy calculation — Tam Holloway's insistence the city must remain free nearly derailed it
- The party's goal: reach the Queen, pursue a peaceful resolution

**Current state:** Party knows the location and method to open a Shadow Road portal to the Shadow Fey Realm. They are headed there.
