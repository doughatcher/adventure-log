The party had spent a month away from Zobeck — the city under its quiet fey occupation, the gates still watched by shadow-touched guards who relocated inconvenient people rather than harming them. When they returned to the problem it was with fresh levels and the particular clarity of people who have had time to think about what they had agreed to. The ambassador's house was behind them. The Winter and Summer Courts were ahead. Between those two points lay the Shadow Road: the route Glanen Thelamandryne had described as the dangerous one, the way mortals traveled when they lacked access to the fey's own transit infrastructure. The party had learned the how and the where before the break. They made the portal. It opened.

Whatever the Shadow Road is in fey cosmology, it is not a place that tolerates hesitation. The party encountered resistance during the crossing — opponents whose specific nature the session did not produce for the record, but whose difficulty level landed in the range the DM afterward described as "formidable." The kind of difficult that tests resource allocation and luck in equal measure. At some point in the fighting, Aldric Voss's relationship with fire turned on him — the wizard died by his own fireball, briefly and loudly. His companions raised him. The exact mechanism of both events — the dying and the raising — remained subjects of post-session commentary rather than precise documentation. What is recorded is this: Portent, his surviving companions observed, is considerably less useful when the problem originates from within.

They came through. The Shadow Road delivered what it advertised: passage to the Shadow Fey realm, earned rather than granted, at a cost measured in fire and resources rather than blood that couldn't be recalled. Session 5 ended with the party across the threshold and into something they had been working toward since the novice burst into the tavern in Zobeck with news about the Temple of Kors. The bard who had once moved through the city's streets sowing confusion — who had turned Magnolia's hand against her own companions while Judith chose the bard's life over the easy retaliation — is dead. The ambassador is at arm's length in a city behind them. What is ahead is the court itself.

The season in the Shadow Fey realm is Summer, which means the Queen holds authority. That season is transitioning — the Shadow Fey's calendar does not track the Material Realm's, and the Winter King's claim on power is approaching if not yet arrived. The party has come to negotiate, and they are negotiating in a window. Tam's instinct to push back hard on fey authority has already made one powerful interlocutor uncomfortable. At court, those instincts will cost more. The party arrived at the edge of the conversation they traveled here to have.
