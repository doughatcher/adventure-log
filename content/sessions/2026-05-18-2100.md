title: Poison and Rebels

The city delivered its welcome with iron and poison. Three bandits with darkened blades came at the party in close quarters — a dirty, efficient ambush that put everyone on the stone except Lyvriele Enafrya, who kept her feet and kept them breathing. When the blades were still and the bodies were cooling, the party found what they had come looking for: rebels, alive and somewhere in the tangle of the Sea of Governance's lower streets.
