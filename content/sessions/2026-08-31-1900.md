Session 7 was originally scheduled for 2026-08-17, then deferred again on 2026-08-24. When the party finally returned to the Shadow Fey court, they were not admitted upward by grandeur. They were sent sideways, into the lower workings of the palace, where rank had to be accumulated one little indignity at a time.

The court's economy proved as practical as it was absurd. Dueling could raise a person's standing, but losing a duel could lower it just as quickly, and Tam Holloway found the math unpleasant. Less glamorous work offered another path. Magnolia Reed honored her promise to the kitchens, burning magic into goodberries only to watch the head cook crush them into soup. Judith and Tam tested the edges of the lower court. Aldric Voss was dragged before King Mago to answer for the wardstone he had broken on the Shadow Road with his regrettable fireball, and after inspection he repaired and improved it with a spell rather than paying in coin.

That repair bought Mago's trust, and the goblin king promptly put Aldric to work hearing cases. A mute goblin named Billoc had been accused of theft; once his hands were freed and the party realized he was trying to sign his defense, Aldric argued that no evidence had proved the charge. Billoc was released with the king's usual moral precision: no stealing from goblins, apparently, but the upper court remained fair prey. For this service Aldric earned the title Reader of the Lower Court. It suited him better than it had any right to.

Tam found a different opening in the stables. The Black Prince's blue warhorse, Lazulin, occupied the finest pen and wanted no nonsense from strangers. The hunting dogs were more approachable, especially once Tam offered meat and the spellwork let them understand him. Their kennel master, Kolya, explained that the Black Prince's hunts are high-court affairs, staged with conjured quarry and courtly prestige. Count Samus Hollowgrand, Master Summoner and a man with more titles than restraint, supplies the prey. The information gave the party a possible route upward: earn enough standing to join or influence the hunt.

By the end of the night the party had improved its footing without drawing blood. Aldric and Magnolia stood highest in the lower court, Tam and Judith had clawed upward behind them, and Billoc and Kolya became names worth remembering. The Summer Queen remained above them, the Winter transition still moving somewhere overhead. The palace had shown them its rule plainly: status is a door, and every door wants payment.

**Key moments**

- Aldric repaired and improved the broken Shadow Road wardstone, paying his fireball debt to Ten-Pound Hammer and King Mago.
- Aldric served as King Mago's Reader of the Lower Court and successfully defended Billoc, a mute goblin accused of stealing.
- Billoc was freed and may become an ally if the party can find him again.
- Magnolia kept her word to the kitchens and supplied fifty goodberries, which were promptly turned into soup.
- Tam befriended the Black Prince's hunting dogs and met Kolya, the kennel master.
- The party learned that Lazulin, the large blue horse in the stables, belongs to the Black Prince.
- Kolya described the Black Prince's hunts and named Count Samus Hollowgrand as the Master Summoner who conjures quarry for them.
- The party identified the hunt as a possible high-court path, though they still need enough status to enter that circle.
- Shadow Fey soldiers from Allosis were identified in the guard hall, though the party learned little beyond their origin and role.

**NPCs introduced or developed**

**King Mago** remains the center of lower-court authority: crude, direct, and less foolish than his language suggests. He accepted Aldric's repaired wardstone, used him to settle court business, and granted him standing for work well done.

**Ten-Pound Hammer** pressed Aldric over the broken wardstone. Once Aldric restored it beyond its original function, the blacksmith accepted the result and left satisfied.

**Billoc** is a mute goblin pickpocket accused of stealing from goblins. The party helped free him after understanding his signed defense. He thanked them and promised they would not regret it, which in the Shadow Fey realm is either a blessing or a warning with manners.

**Kolya** is the kennel master for the Black Prince's hunting dogs. He responded well to Tam's easy rapport with the animals and explained enough of the hunt to make it useful.

**Lazulin** is the Black Prince's massive blue horse. The dogs describe him as dangerous; Tam introduced himself from a respectful distance, which was wise and therefore suspiciously unlike the evening's general trend.

**Count Samus Hollowgrand** is the Master Summoner associated with the Black Prince's hunt, also named as royal standard bearer, herald, master of defense, lord of fallen archers, and lord of broken swords. The title pile is not subtle.
