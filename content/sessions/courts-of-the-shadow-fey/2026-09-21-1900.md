Third time was the charm. Session 8 had already survived a Labor Day bye week and a sick call before it finally happened, landing the party two days into a week-long dueling season at court — a tradition with its own house rules for the occasion. No armor, no shields on the sand, and winning raises a duelist's standing. Losing does not forgive the healers who patch you back up afterward: any spell slot burned on healing during dueling season only refunds at half strength on a long rest. The court does not make its charity cheap.

Magnolia spent much of the week in the infirmary paying that price, stitching duelists back together as they cycled through. One case stood apart from ordinary bruises — a fighter carrying what looked like a poison working in concert with something colder, a necrotic aftertaste that didn't behave like simple venom. Suspicion in the infirmary settled on one name almost immediately: Vaelith Graveshade.

The dueling grounds have their own hierarchy. The Ravens are the court's standing duelist faction, and word has it Judith runs with them, whatever her actual membership status. Vaelith Graveshade is not one of them — an outsider even among duelists, which may explain what happened next. Judith drew Vaelith in the ring and won cleanly. Vaelith did not take it as sport. She hissed that no one could know her secret and then went for a kill that had nothing to do with dueling rules. Judith put her down without killing her, and she and a companion named Joshah hauled the unconscious woman out of the grounds and threw her out of court entirely. Whatever the secret was, it left with her, unconfirmed.

Away from the blood sand, quieter business moved just as fast. A young goblin named Amerilis found Aldric and made an odd request on behalf of Princess Vailessia — an eight-year-old ward of the Summer Court currently living in the Winter Palace — who apparently wants nothing in the world so much as a puppy. Aldric and Tam took the request to Kolya, the kennel master they'd already built rapport with over the hunting hounds, and picked the runt of a litter to send along to the princess.

Chasing the errand turned up more than a puppy. Tam picked up court gossip about a Lady Sariel: a moody, black-clad figure who wanders the Winter Palace halls with all the sullen weather of a teenager, in the care of one Count Sammas Hallowgrin — Master Summoner, Royal Standard Bearer and Herald, Master of Fence, Lord of Fallen Archways and Broken Swords, and the same conjurer of hunt-quarry the party already knew as "Count Samus Hollowgrand" — Vargr confirmed in-table it's the same man and Hallowgrin is the correct spelling. Sariel, it seems, is fixated on the kennel master's troubles — some slight against the dogs that has yet to be explained. And somewhere above all of it sits Revich, an all-seeing angel said to keep residence in the Winter Palace's Tower of Light — a name new to the record and untested so far.
