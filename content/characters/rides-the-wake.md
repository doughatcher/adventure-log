Rides the Wake carries herself like someone accustomed to moving through rough water — patient in stillness, overwhelming in motion.

She is not reckless in the way people imagine barbarians to be. There is a current of deliberate purpose beneath the fury, a sense that the rage, when it comes, has been earned. Those who travel with her describe a quiet confidence that borders on the serene, right up until it doesn't. The name fits: she goes where the force has been and holds the line in the turbulence that follows.

Shard C took her down for good. The Grim Reaper — a name that turned out to be a description — had already carved through her once, and came back to finish the accounting. Piercing, poison, the cold arithmetic of something that does not tire and does not negotiate. She had taken hits that would have felled others. She had held long enough for the party to draw breath, to strike back, to nearly turn it. The Healing Word bought seconds. The seconds were not enough. In the end, what killed her was not recklessness, not carelessness, but the simple truth that even the wake has an edge — and she rode it all the way there.

She went down swinging, in the company of people who called her teammate and meant it. Whatever comes next for those who die inside a Shard, she did not die small.
