Rides the Wake carried herself like someone accustomed to moving through rough water — patient in stillness, overwhelming in motion.

She was not reckless in the way people imagine barbarians to be. There was a current of deliberate purpose beneath the fury, a sense that the rage, when it came, had been earned. Those who traveled with her described a quiet confidence that bordered on the serene, right up until it didn't. The name fit: she went where the force had been and held the line in the turbulence that followed.

Shard C claimed her. The same relentless arithmetic that had found her before found her again — piercing and poison stacking faster than anyone could answer — and this time there was no Healing Word quick enough, no margin left to borrow against. She went down fighting, which was the only way she knew how to go down. The others called her name in the aftermath, and the silence that answered was a different kind of water entirely. Someone said it was Leonard who landed the final blow, speaking the name like it mattered to assign one — like the thing that kills you should at least have something to answer to. It should be said that she did not go quietly. She announced it herself: *I'm going down and I'm taking you with me.* The current had other plans.

She is gone now. What remains is the shape of the space she occupied — the flank she held, the hits she absorbed, the particular quality of recklessness-that-wasn't that the party will notice most acutely in the next fight, when someone needs to stand in the bad place and there is one fewer person willing to do it.
