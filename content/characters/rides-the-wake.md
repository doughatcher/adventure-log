Rides the Wake carried herself like someone accustomed to moving through rough water — patient in stillness, overwhelming in motion.

She was not reckless in the way people imagine barbarians to be. There was a current of deliberate purpose beneath the fury, a sense that the rage, when it came, had been earned. Those who traveled with her described a quiet confidence that bordered on the serene, right up until it didn't. The name fit: she went where the force had been and held the line in the turbulence that followed.

Shard C nearly claimed her. The same relentless arithmetic that had found her before found her again — piercing and poison stacking faster than anyone could answer — and the margin became thin enough to have a name. She went down fighting, which was the only way she knew how to go down. The others called her name in the aftermath, and this time there was still an answer.

What remains is not absence, but the party's memory of how narrow survival can be. Rides the Wake did not go quietly because she did not go. She remains the person willing to stand in the bad place, the anchor in the line, the wave that has not yet reached the end of the shore.
