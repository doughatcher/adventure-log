The only non-human in a party of humans, which has never seemed to particularly define her but does mean she notices things they don't. An elven druid of the Circle of Dreams — fey-adjacent by nature, which gives her a complicated relationship with the Shadow Fey occupation of Zobeck: less inclined toward outrage, more attuned to the particular grammar of how fey power operates. In the ambassador's negotiation, Magnolia positioned herself as the pragmatist — civilian safety over political outcomes, working within the power structure rather than against it. That instinct continues to serve her.

The kitchens are not where most adventurers look for political leverage. Magnolia made a promise to the kitchen staff earlier in the palace and kept it — fifty goodberries, delivered and crushed into soup by the head cook. In a court where status is currency, that act of follow-through bought something real. There is a version of this story where the promise gets quietly forgotten in favor of more obviously consequential things. She did not tell that version.

She holds status 7 alongside Aldric, and while his came from a courtroom, hers came from a kitchen — which may say something about where power actually lives in a place like this. The Shadow Fey court runs on obligation honored and reputation maintained. Magnolia seems to have understood that before the others did.
