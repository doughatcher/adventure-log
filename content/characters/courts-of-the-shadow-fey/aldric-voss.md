A human wizard who approaches the arcane with enough confidence to sometimes outpace his own caution. Divination gives him Portent — the ability to roll dice at dawn and slot predetermined results into moments that matter. This did not help him during the Shadow Road crossing, when a fireball of his own making briefly ended his participation in the journey. He was revived. His companions noted, with the restraint of people who have been through a lot together, that Portent applies to *others'* dice, not to consequences one has authored.

At the Shadow Fey palace, Aldric faced the accounting for that fireball directly. Summoned before King Mago over the destroyed wardstone, he did not deflect or apologize his way through it — he inspected the damage, understood what the stone actually was, and repaired it. Improved it. Ten-Pound Hammer accepted the work. This is the best possible outcome from a problem you caused yourself, and Aldric reached it through exactly the scholarly instinct that makes him useful: when confronted with something broken, understand it first, then fix it.

The appointment as Reader of the Lower Court followed immediately, which is either an honor or a burden depending on how the next case goes. The case of Billoc went well. A mute goblin, hands bound, accused of theft and unable to defend himself — Aldric recognized what was wrong with that picture, helped free the goblin's hands so he could sign, and made the argument to Mago that absence of proof is not proof of guilt. Billoc walked free. In a court built on status and performance, that kind of visible justice earns something real. Aldric currently holds status 7 in the lower court, the highest in the party, and it was earned in a courtroom rather than a duel.

**At a glance.** Level 8 Divination Wizard, INT 18, Ritual Adept (Detect Magic, Identify, Comprehend Languages cast from the book). The party started this campaign on 2500gp and one free uncommon item apiece; Magnolia took a Moon Sickle +1 and Tam an Amulet of Proof against Detection and Location. Aldric's starting item was never posted to the channel.
