A human ranger with a Fey Wanderer's subclass and, somewhat at odds with that heritage, a deep and vocal resistance to the idea that any city should trade its freedom for the Fey Courts' version of order. That disposition has not softened inside the palace. When the party encountered soldiers from Allosis under Captain Memorethian in the guard hall, Tam's read of the room was quick — he knows the shape of an occupying force when he sees one, and seeing it here, inside the court itself, does not seem to have made him more comfortable with any of this.

The stables were more his element. Tam found the Black Prince's hunting dogs with meat and animal speech — exactly the kind of approach that works when status and courtly maneuvering have their limits. Go to the creatures, speak their language, earn trust the direct way. Through Kolya the kennel master he learned that the Black Prince's hunt is a high-court event, that the quarry is conjured by Count Samus Hollowgrand, and that the large blue horse named Lazulin belongs to the Black Prince himself. Information gathered not through intrigue but through patience and a piece of meat given freely.

He has also taken careful note of dueling culture — that rank can be won through combat, and lost through it. Tam filed that away without committing to it. He is a ranger who has seen enough real fighting to be unimpressed by arenas, and enough court politics to know that a duel entered too early is a resource spent before you understand the board. His status sits at five. He is watching.
