A human ranger with a Fey Wanderer's subclass and, somewhat at odds with that heritage, a deep and vocal resistance to the idea that any city should trade its freedom for the Fey Courts' version of order. That disposition has not softened inside the palace. When the party probed the guard hall and encountered soldiers from Allosis under Captain Memorethian, Tam's read of the room was quick — he knows the shape of an occupying force when he sees one, and seeing it here, inside the court itself, does not seem to have made him more comfortable with any of this.

The stables were more his element. Tam found the Black Prince's hunting dogs with meat and animal speech — exactly the kind of approach that works when status and courtly maneuvering have their limits. Go to the creatures, speak their language, earn trust the direct way. Through Kolya the kennel master he learned that the Black Prince's hunt is a high-court event, that the quarry is conjured by Count Samus Hollowgrand, and that the large blue horse named Lazulin belongs to the Black Prince himself. Useful intelligence, gathered without a single persuasion check.

He also tested the court's dueling culture and learned the stakes quickly: rank can be won in a duel, but rank can also be lost, and Tam is not the kind of man who auditions for things he isn't ready to finish. Status 5 for now, shared with Judith, which in this court means there is still room to move — upward, if the right opportunity presents itself. He seems to be looking for one that doesn't require him to become something he isn't.
