The name is doing a lot of work and it is doing all of it correctly. A human rogue who moves like someone who has thought carefully about when to use a blade and when to use a word, and who has arrived at the conclusion that both have their place and the choice is mostly about timing.

When the Shadow Fey bard turned Magnolia's hand against the party, Judith had a choice: retaliate against Magnolia, or take the fight to the bard. She killed the bard. The party survived intact. This is the kind of decision that doesn't get made explicitly — it happens in the space of a round, in the prioritization of a Swashbuckler's movement and action. It says something about her.

At the ambassador's meeting she was part of a negotiation that produced "barely friends" as an outcome. That is a better result than it sounds.
