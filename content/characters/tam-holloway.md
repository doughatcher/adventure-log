A human ranger with a Fey Wanderer's subclass and, somewhat at odds with that heritage, a deep and vocal resistance to the idea that any city should trade its freedom for the Fey Courts' version of order. In the ambassador's office, Tam pushed hard on the sovereignty of Zobeck — harder than the room wanted, hard enough that the negotiation started sliding before Magnolia's pragmatism and the group's collective commitment to not dying retrieved it.

The Fey Wanderer path gives Tam a particular fluency with fey creatures and fey magic. That fluency does not appear to have softened his political views about fey governance. This will be relevant at court.

Whether Tam's stance will evolve at the Shadow Fey court, or harden further at the sight of the Queen's actual authority, is one of the more interesting questions the campaign has set up.
