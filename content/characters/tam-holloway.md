Tam Holloway is the human ranger played by Ryan.

His position on the shadow fey is unambiguous: the Free City of Zobeck should remain free. He pressed this hard enough during the ambassador meeting that the negotiations nearly collapsed. Glanen Thelamandryne did not appreciate it. The party pulled it back from the brink, but the dynamic is established.

This will matter in the Shadow Fey Courts, where bluntness about fey sovereignty is likely to land worse than it does with a single ambassador.
