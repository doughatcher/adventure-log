## Setting

The published Kobold Press adventure *Courts of the Shadow Fey*, run in the Free
City of Zobeck and the Shadow Realm beyond it.

Zobeck was a monarchy until roughly a century ago, when the line died out or was
overthrown — the record is not clear which, and the ambiguity is the point. It
has been a free city since. The dispute the campaign turns on is this: the old
monarchy held its right to rule by grant of the Fey Courts, and with the
monarchy gone the Shadow Fey have come to reclaim what they granted.

The occupation has been deliberate rather than violent. Shadow Fey replaced the
city guard at the gates; anyone who interferes is teleported elsewhere in the
city rather than harmed. This is a demonstration of control, not a slaughter,
and reading it as the latter is a mistake the party has already avoided once.

### The Courts

The Shadow Realm runs two courts that trade power by season, on a calendar
independent of the Material Realm's:

- **Summer Court** — the Queen. Currently in session.
- **Winter Court** — the King. His claim rises as the season turns.

The transition from Summer to Winter is in progress. The party's negotiating
window with the Queen is contracting, which is the campaign's clock.

Beneath both sits a **lower court**, at the palace service level, presided over
by King Mago — a goblin king whose authority is real but bounded. Court rank is
currency, and access to the higher tiers is status-gated. The party is working
that ladder.

## Campaign Tone & Rules

From the DM, pre-campaign: "While there will be combat in the campaign, you're
interacting with the High Courts of the Shadow Realm. Be sure you have other
tools in your toolset, and other skills that could be useful. Without giving too
much away there will be some tricky challenges to overcome that are more than
just 'smash with big sword'."

This is a social and political campaign with combat in it, not the reverse.
Status, promises kept, and who owes whom are the mechanics that matter.

## Key NPCs

**Glanen Thelamandryne** — Shadow Fey ambassador to Zobeck. Received the party
at his residence after inviting them via cards left on their pillows while they
slept. Explained the court structure and the Shadow Road. Standing with the
party: "barely friends." Nasty dogs at his door. An owl on a stand in his office
was a fey in disguise and gifted the party bracers of defense.

**King Mago** — Goblin king of the lower court. Appointed Aldric Reader of the
Lower Court after the wardstone repair. Persuadable when the argument is sound;
he freed Billoc on the party's reasoning.

**Billoc** — Mute goblin, accused of theft, freed by Aldric's advocacy when the
party argued that a man whose only language is his hands cannot defend himself
with them bound. Grateful. Location unknown since.

**Ten-Pound Hammer** — Goblin blacksmith. Inspected and approved Aldric's
wardstone repair. Debt resolved; relationship neutral-positive.

**Kolya** — Kennel master to the Black Prince's hunting hounds. Warmed to Tam.
Named Lazulin, the Black Prince's great blue horse. The party's best route to
the hunt.

**Count Samus Hollowgrand** — Master Summoner who conjures the quarry for the
Black Prince's hunt. Not yet contacted.

**Captain Memorethian** — Commands the Allosis Shadow Fey soldiers in the guard
hall. Observed only; no relationship established.

**Oleg** — High priest of the Temple of Kors in Zobeck. Survived the assassins.
His situation back in the material world is unresolved.

## Session Log

**Sessions 1–3 (2026-06-01, 2026-06-08, 2026-06-15):** No post-session notes captured. Party established in Zobeck, Shadow Fey occupation of the city began, temple of Kors attacked by Shadow Fey assassins (high priest Oleg protected). Shadow Fey bard encountered — bard mind-controlled Magnolia Reed, who attacked the party; Judith killed the bard rather than retaliating against her companion. Research and diplomacy phase undertaken.

**Session 4 (2026-06-22):** Party met with the Shadow Fey ambassador Glanen Thelamandryne at his residence. Tense negotiation produced a fragile "barely friends" status. Owl in the ambassador's office revealed as fey in disguise — gifted party bracers of defense. Ambassador explained the fey political structure (Summer Court: Queen currently in power; Winter Court: King; Shadow Fey seasons independent of Material Realm calendar) and how to access the Shadow Realm via the Shadow Road. Party determined to make the crossing. Campaign paused ~4 weeks (Vargr and Richard both unavailable).

**Session 5 (2026-07-27):** Party leveled to 8 and crossed into the Shadow Fey realm via the Shadow Road. Portal successfully created and opened. Formidable combat encountered during the crossing; foes not documented. Aldric Voss died by self-inflicted fireball and was revived. Party arrived in the Shadow Fey realm with the Summer Court in session and the seasonal transition toward Winter in progress.

**Session 6 (2026-08-03):** Party entered the Shadow Fey Summer Court at Level 8 to seek audience with the Summer Queen. Seasonal transition toward the Winter King was in progress, narrowing the party's negotiating window. No audio capture available; player debrief pending. Session log archived as stub — to be updated when notes arrive.

**Session 7 (2026-08-31):** Party worked the Shadow Fey lower court rather than forcing its way upward. Aldric answered for the wardstone he damaged on the Shadow Road, repaired it with magic, and earned King Mago's trust enough to serve as Reader of the Lower Court. In that role he helped free Billoc, a mute goblin accused of theft, by recognizing that the bound prisoner could not defend himself without his hands. Magnolia honored her kitchen promise and turned spell slots into goodberry soup for the court. Tam found a safer ladder through the stables, befriending the Black Prince's hunting dogs and meeting kennel master Kolya. The party learned that the Black Prince's hunt, with Lazulin the blue horse and Count Samus Hollowgrand's conjured quarry, may be a path toward higher status and eventually the Queen.
